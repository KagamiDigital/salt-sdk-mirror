    OptionalonPaymentRequired

    onPaymentRequired?: OnPaymentRequired

    Called with the terms the 402 asked for, once they're chosen and before anything is signed. The wallet paying must be on PaymentTerms.chainId — it refuses typed data whose domain names a chain it isn't on — and this is the only point at which that chain is known and still switchable. Throw to abort the payment. Ignored without a payer.

    organisationId

    organisationId: string

    The organisation this account belongs to

    Optionalpayer

    payer?: ViemTypedDataSignerLike

    Signer for the x402 payment that gates POST /accounts. Needn't be signer; any wallet that can sign an EIP-3009 TransferWithAuthorization may pay on the creator's behalf. Omit when supplying a payment signed ahead of time, or when the backend doesn't charge for account creation; a 402 with neither surfaces as PaymentRequiredError.

    Optionalpayment

    payment?: SignedPayment

    A payment signed ahead of time with Salt.signPayment, so creation costs one request instead of a 402 and a retry. Supply a payer as well to fall back to signing reactively if this one is refused — it expires, and the price it quotes can move.
